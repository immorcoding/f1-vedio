# Shape inbox: feat/mv-yas-perspective

Signals from rebuilding the Yas hotel in shot 4.1 (the Abu Dhabi title card). The user said the gridshell's proportion and perspective look strange, but liked the grandstand canopy.

## Art direction

- 2026-10-04 · ART-9 broken by code, now fixed: the hotel was a side elevation traced from a photo, pasted flat and facing the camera at 0.5 m per photo px. That made it about 60 m tall, against a real gridshell height of 35 m. It is now 3D geometry in world metres, projected through the scene's `CAM`. Each block is a box with 2.7 m storeys. Each gridshell is a swept section whose diamond lattice lives on the surface, so the diamonds foreshorten and converge at the shell ends. Back-facing cells draw as the lit underside. The bridge is a frontal plane over the track.
- 2026-10-04 · ART-4 decided part of the change: the plan comes from the OSM footprint (relation 2541470), and the dimensions come from the structural and facade engineers (sbp: 217 × 44 × 35 m; Front Inc.: 2.5–3.5 m panels). The brief's "85 m wide" turned out to be the 85,000 m² floor area. Its "40–45 m tall" does not match the 35 m shell height, so the 35 m figure was used. Bridge height and block height are not published, so they are estimates (see facts.md).
- 2026-10-04 · ART-9 friction: the real west block is square to the track. Drawn that way, it would face the camera flat, which is the look the user rejected. It is turned 25° (to 65° from the view axis), a composite liberty on the same footing as the compressed lap. Proposal: say in ART-9 that a composite may rotate a real building to read in depth, as long as its proportions and its relation to the track stay true, and that the rotation is recorded in facts.md.
- 2026-10-04 · ART-9 consequence (needs a user look): at true scale and the existing distance (bridge 183 m down the straight), the hotel is much smaller than before. Its crown sits at about y 545, below the floodlight lamps, and the pit building hides the lower half of the west block. Bringing it closer would mean moving the right-hand floodlight towers at z 118/156, which stand where the east block's podium would be.
- 2026-10-04 · ART-9 decided part of the change: the walls, fences, run-off and track now run on under the bridge to z = 420 m, instead of stopping at a facade plane. The pit building and pit lane end at the west podium's nearest corner, which YasHotel exports as `HOTEL_NEAR`. This replaces the earlier inbox proposal that elements along the view axis end where the closing element stands: with the blocks either side of the track, nothing closes the straight.
